# E-Commerce Platform

## Overview

The E-Commerce Platform is a full-stack web application designed to provide a complete online shopping experience. The system supports customer registration, product browsing, shopping cart management, checkout processing, order tracking, and administrative product management.

The platform was developed to demonstrate modern web application development practices, combining a React frontend with backend services and database-driven functionality.
