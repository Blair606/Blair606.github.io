# E-Learning Management System

## Overview

The E-Learning Management System is a database-driven educational platform designed to support students, teachers, parents, and administrators within a unified learning environment.

The system supports online learning through course management, assessments, discussion groups, progress monitoring, reporting, and analytics.

This project demonstrates experience in frontend development, backend integration, relational database design, and educational workflow management.
